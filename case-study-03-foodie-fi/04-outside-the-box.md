# Outside-the-Box Questions

[← Back to Foodie-Fi overview](./README.md)

These questions are open-ended and focus on applying technical analysis to business decisions.

## 1. How would I calculate Foodie-Fi's growth rate?

Foodie-Fi should measure growth using both customers and revenue.

### Customer growth

```text
Customer growth rate =
(Ending customers - Beginning customers)
÷ Beginning customers × 100
```

### Monthly recurring revenue growth

```text
MRR growth rate =
(Ending MRR - Beginning MRR)
÷ Beginning MRR × 100
```

### Additional measures

- Gross new customers
- Churned customers
- Net customer growth
- New MRR
- Expansion MRR from upgrades
- Contraction MRR from downgrades
- Churned MRR
- Net revenue retention

Customer growth should be analysed by acquisition cohort because strong new-customer acquisition can hide poor retention.

## 3. Which customer journeys should be analysed further?

The following journeys could provide useful retention insights:

### Trial to churn

Compare engagement levels between customers who converted and customers who churned immediately.

### Trial to paid plan

Identify the actions customers perform before converting.

### Basic to pro

Determine which product features encourage upgrades.

### Monthly to annual

Identify when customers become confident enough to make a longer commitment.

### Paid plan to churn

Analyse usage and engagement changes before cancellation.

### Failed or incomplete payments

Identify whether involuntary churn is caused by payment failures rather than customer intent.

### Downgrade journeys

Understand whether customers downgrade because of price, low usage or missing features.

## 4. What should be included in an exit survey?

A short cancellation survey could ask:

1. What is the main reason you are cancelling?
2. How frequently did you use Foodie-Fi?
3. Was the subscription good value for money?
4. Which features did you use most?
5. Which features or content were missing?
6. Did you experience any technical problems?
7. Was the available content relevant to you?
8. Which competing service will you use instead?
9. Would a cheaper plan change your decision?
10. Would you consider returning in the future?
11. What could Foodie-Fi have done to retain you?

Most questions should use predefined options, with one optional free-text field.

## 5. Which business levers could reduce churn?

### Improve trial onboarding

Show customers relevant content immediately and explain the value of paid features.

### Personalise content recommendations

Use viewing behaviour to recommend content that is relevant to each customer.

### Improve annual-plan positioning

Offer annual-plan savings after customers demonstrate consistent monthly usage.

### Introduce cancellation alternatives

Offer options such as:

- Pause subscription
- Downgrade plan
- Temporary discount
- Reduced-price retention offer

### Re-engage inactive customers

Trigger personalised reminders when usage drops.

### Improve content quality

Use searches, viewing history and abandoned content to identify gaps in the catalogue.

### Reduce involuntary churn

Send payment reminders and automatically retry failed payments.

## Validation Approach

Each retention initiative should be tested using:

1. A clearly defined customer group
2. A control group
3. A treatment group
4. A measurable success metric
5. A defined test period
6. Statistical-significance testing
7. Guardrail metrics

Possible success metrics include:

- Higher trial conversion
- Lower churn
- Higher engagement
- Higher annual-plan adoption
- Higher customer lifetime value
- Improved net revenue retention

The analysis should also confirm that a short-term reduction in churn does not create excessive discounting or reduce long-term revenue.
